Campus Information - Columban College, Inc.

Library Hours and Services
The Columban College, Inc. Library is open Monday through Friday from 8:00 AM to 10:00 PM, Saturday from 10:00 AM to 8:00 PM, and Sunday from 12:00 PM to 8:00 PM. During final exam weeks, the library extends its hours to 24/7 access. Students can check out up to 10 books at a time for a period of 3 weeks. The library offers study rooms that can be reserved online through the library portal. Printing services cost $0.10 per black and white page and $0.50 per color page.

Student Dining Services
The main dining hall, University Commons, serves breakfast from 7:00 AM to 10:00 AM, lunch from 11:30 AM to 2:00 PM, and dinner from 5:00 PM to 8:00 PM. Meal plans are required for all first-year students living on campus. The campus has three meal plan options: the Basic Plan (12 meals per week), the Standard Plan (15 meals per week), and the Unlimited Plan (unlimited meals). There are also four cafes across campus that accept dining dollars: Java Junction in the Science Building, The Corner Cafe in the Student Union, Brew & Bites in the Library, and Quick Eats near the Athletic Center.

Registration and Academic Calendar
Fall semester registration opens on April 1st for current students. New student orientation occurs the week before classes begin. The fall semester runs from late August to mid-December, with a one-week fall break in October. Spring semester runs from mid-January to early May, with a one-week spring break in March. Students must maintain a minimum GPA of 2.0 to remain in good academic standing. Course add/drop period lasts for the first two weeks of each semester.

Housing Information
All first-year students are required to live on campus. Housing applications open on March 1st each year. There are five residence halls on campus: Oakwood Hall (traditional dorms), Maple Hall (suite-style), Elm Hall (apartment-style), Pine Hall (honors housing), and Cedar Hall (quiet/study-focused). Room and board costs range from $8,000 to $12,000 per academic year depending on the housing type. Students can apply for housing exemptions if they live within 30 miles of campus with family.

Parking and Transportation
Parking permits are required for all vehicles on campus. Student parking permits cost $150 per semester for general parking lots and $300 per semester for premium lots near academic buildings. The campus shuttle runs every 15 minutes from 7:00 AM to 11:00 PM on weekdays and every 30 minutes on weekends. The shuttle has four routes: Red Route (academic buildings), Blue Route (residence halls), Green Route (athletic facilities), and Yellow Route (off-campus apartments). All students ride the shuttle for free with their student ID.

Health and Wellness Center
The Student Health Center is located in the Wellness Building and is open Monday through Friday from 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM. Students pay a health services fee each semester that covers basic medical services, but prescriptions and specialized treatments may have additional costs. Mental health counseling is available through the Counseling Center with appointments available Monday through Friday. Students can schedule up to 12 free counseling sessions per academic year.

Campus Recreation and Fitness
The Athletic Center is open from 6:00 AM to 11:00 PM daily. All students have free access to the fitness center, indoor track, swimming pool, and basketball courts. Group fitness classes including yoga, spin, and HIIT are offered throughout the week at no additional cost. Students can rent equipment such as tennis rackets, basketballs, and yoga mats from the equipment desk with their student ID. Intramural sports leagues run each semester and include soccer, basketball, volleyball, and flag football.

Student Support Services
The Academic Success Center offers free tutoring in math, science, writing, and foreign languages. Tutoring is available on a walk-in basis Monday through Thursday from 3:00 PM to 9:00 PM and by appointment. The Career Services Office helps students with resume writing, interview preparation, and job searching. Career counselors are available Monday through Friday from 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM. Students can schedule appointments through the career services portal.

Financial Aid and Bursar
The Financial Aid Office is located in the Administration Building, Room 102. Office hours are Monday through Friday from 8:30 AM to 4:30 PM. Students must submit the FAFSA by March 1st each year to be considered for financial aid. Tuition payment deadlines are August 1st for fall semester and December 15th for spring semester. The university offers payment plans that allow students to spread tuition payments over 4 months with a $50 enrollment fee.

Campus Safety and Emergency Contacts
Campus Safety can be reached 24/7 at extension 911 from any campus phone or (555) 123-4567 from cell phones. Emergency blue light phones are located throughout campus and directly connect to Campus Safety dispatch. The SafeWalk escort service is available from 8:00 PM to 2:00 AM every day for students who want an escort to their car or residence hall. Students can request a SafeWalk by calling Campus Safety or using the campus safety mobile app.
