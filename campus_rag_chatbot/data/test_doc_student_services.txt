WELCOME TO STUDENT SERVICES

The Office of Student Affairs (OSA) coordinates all student-related services across the Main Campus. Located on the second floor of the Administration Building (Room AD-201), the OSA is your first stop for questions about student organizations, campus activities, and disciplinary matters.

HEALTH AND WELLNESS

The school clinic is available for students who feel unwell during class hours. Our Health Clinic is staffed by a registered nurse and is located at the Student Center, ground floor (SC-G05). For mental health concerns or academic counseling, visit the Guidance and Counseling Office nearby at SC-G06. The guidance counselors provide career advice, study tips, and confidential support.

ENROLLMENT AND RECORDS

For enrollment concerns, transcript requests, or grade inquiries, proceed to the Registrar's Office at the Administration Building ground floor (AD-101). New students should visit the Admissions Office (AD-102) for application status and enrollment requirements. The Cashier's window (AD-103) handles all payment transactions including tuition, miscellaneous fees, and clearance payments.

CAMPUS DINING

The main cafeteria (also called the canteen or food court) is located at the Student Center ground floor. Hot meals, snacks, and beverages are available from 7:00 AM to 6:00 PM. The dining hall also serves as a common area for students between classes.

BOOKSTORE

School supplies, textbooks, and official uniforms can be purchased at the College Bookstore in the Student Center (SC-G02). Note that the old bookstore location in Academic Building A has been closed and is now used for storage.
