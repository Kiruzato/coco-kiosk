Welcome to Columban College! This memo will help you find the key offices and facilities you'll need during your first semester.

FIRST THINGS FIRST

1. ENROLLMENT
   If you haven't completed enrollment, go to the Registrar's Office (Admin Bldg, Ground Floor). For questions about admission requirements, the Admissions Office is right next door.

2. PAYMENT
   After enrollment, pay your tuition at the Cashier (also called the finance office or payment window). Bring your assessment form.

3. ID AND ACCOUNTS
   Visit the ICT office (some call it the IT Office or tech support) at Academic Building B, 3rd Floor to get your student email and WiFi credentials. Engineering students should go to the IT Office at the Engineering Campus instead.

DAILY NEEDS

- FOOD: The cafeteria/canteen/food court is at the Student Center. Open 7 AM to 6 PM.
- BOOKS: Buy textbooks at the bookstore (Student Center, beside the kainan). Don't go to the old bookstore - it's closed!
- HEALTH: Feeling sick? The clinic (nurse station) is at Student Center Ground Floor.
- COUNSELING: For academic or personal concerns, the guidance office is near the clinic.

STUDY SPACES

The library (LRC) in Academic Building A, 2nd Floor is your best bet for quiet study. Computer labs are available at Academic Building B if you need to use a PC.

ATHLETICS

PE classes are held at the gymnasium (covered court/sports hall). It's located at the back of campus, past the parking area.

GETTING HELP

For any student concerns:
- Dean's Office (CAS): Academic Building A, 3rd Floor
- Dean's Office (Engineering): Engineering Building, 2nd Floor
- Student Affairs (OSA): Admin Building, 2nd Floor

Questions? Visit the OSA or ask any student assistant.

Good luck with your studies!
