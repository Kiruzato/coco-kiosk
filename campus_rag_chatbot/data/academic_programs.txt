Academic Programs - Columban College, Inc.

Undergraduate Programs

College of Arts and Sciences
Columban College, Inc. offers over 40 undergraduate majors through the College of Arts and Sciences. Popular majors include Biology, Psychology, English Literature, History, and Political Science. Students can also pursue interdisciplinary majors such as Environmental Studies or Cognitive Science. The college requires all students to complete a core curriculum that includes courses in writing, mathematics, natural sciences, social sciences, and humanities. Most majors require between 36 and 48 credit hours of coursework within the major field.

College of Engineering
The College of Engineering offers Bachelor of Science degrees in Civil Engineering, Mechanical Engineering, Electrical Engineering, Computer Engineering, and Chemical Engineering. Engineering students must complete a five-year program that includes a mandatory co-op experience where students work in industry for 6-12 months. The engineering curriculum emphasizes hands-on learning with extensive laboratory courses and senior capstone design projects. Students must maintain a minimum GPA of 2.5 to remain in the engineering program.

School of Business
The School of Business offers BBA degrees in Accounting, Finance, Marketing, Management, and Information Systems. Business students must complete 120 credit hours including 45 hours of general education requirements and 60 hours of business core and major-specific courses. The School requires all students to complete an internship before graduation. Popular specializations include Data Analytics, Entrepreneurship, and International Business. The School of Business is AACSB accredited.

College of Education
The College of Education prepares students for teaching careers in elementary, middle, and secondary education. Programs include Elementary Education, Secondary Education (with subject area concentrations), Special Education, and Early Childhood Education. All education majors must complete student teaching requirements during their senior year, which involves a full semester of supervised teaching in a local school. Graduates are eligible for state teaching certification upon program completion.

Graduate Programs

Master's Programs
Columban College, Inc. offers Master's degrees in over 25 fields including MBA, Master of Public Administration, Master of Education, MS in Computer Science, MS in Data Science, MA in English, and MA in Psychology. Most programs require 30-36 credit hours and can be completed in 1.5 to 2 years of full-time study. Part-time options are available for working professionals. Graduate students must maintain a minimum GPA of 3.0. The MBA program offers evening and weekend classes to accommodate working professionals.

Doctoral Programs
Doctoral programs are offered in Education (EdD), Psychology (PhD), and Computer Science (PhD). PhD programs typically require 60-72 credit hours beyond the bachelor's degree, including dissertation research. Students must pass comprehensive examinations and defend a dissertation to earn the degree. Most PhD students complete their programs in 4-6 years. Full-time PhD students are eligible for teaching assistantships that provide tuition remission and a stipend of $22,000 per academic year.

Certificate Programs
Columban College, Inc. offers graduate certificate programs in Project Management, Data Analytics, Cybersecurity, College Teaching, and Educational Leadership. Certificate programs require 12-15 credit hours and can often be completed in one year of part-time study. Credits earned in certificate programs can typically be applied toward a related master's degree if students choose to continue their education.

Academic Policies

Degree Requirements
Undergraduate students must complete a minimum of 120 credit hours to graduate, with at least 30 credit hours completed at Columban College, Inc.. Students must maintain a cumulative GPA of 2.0 or higher. A minimum grade of C is required in all major courses. Students must complete all general education requirements, which typically account for 40-45 credit hours.

Grading Scale
The university uses a standard letter grading system: A (4.0), A- (3.7), B+ (3.3), B (3.0), B- (2.7), C+ (2.3), C (2.0), C- (1.7), D+ (1.3), D (1.0), and F (0.0). Graduate programs typically require a minimum grade of B in all coursework. Students can elect to take certain courses on a Pass/Fail basis with instructor approval, though major courses cannot be taken Pass/Fail.

Academic Standing
Students must maintain satisfactory academic progress to remain enrolled. Students with a GPA below 2.0 are placed on academic probation. Students on probation have one semester to raise their GPA above 2.0 or face academic suspension. Students may appeal suspensions through the Academic Standards Committee. Academic honors are awarded to students with a GPA of 3.5 or higher (Dean's List) or 3.8 or higher (President's List).

Transfer Credits
Columban College, Inc. accepts transfer credits from regionally accredited institutions. A maximum of 90 credit hours can be transferred toward a bachelor's degree. Transfer courses must have been completed with a grade of C or better. Transfer students must complete at least 30 credit hours at Columban College, Inc. to be eligible for a degree. The Registrar's Office evaluates all transfer credits and provides students with a transfer credit evaluation within 2-3 weeks of admission.

Study Abroad Programs
Columban College, Inc. has partnerships with universities in 20 countries including Spain, France, Italy, Japan, Australia, and Costa Rica. Study abroad programs are available for a semester, academic year, or summer term. Students can earn Columban College, Inc. credit for approved study abroad courses. The Study Abroad Office provides advising and assists students with applications, visas, and logistics. Many study abroad programs include cultural excursions and language immersion opportunities. Financial aid can be applied to study abroad programs.

Academic Support Resources

Tutoring Services
The Academic Success Center provides free peer tutoring in over 30 subjects including mathematics, sciences, writing, foreign languages, and business courses. Tutoring is available on a drop-in basis Monday through Thursday from 3:00 PM to 9:00 PM. Students can also schedule one-on-one tutoring appointments. Group study sessions are offered before midterm and final exams. Professional writing consultants are available to help students with papers, theses, and dissertations at any stage of the writing process.

Academic Advising
All undergraduate students are assigned an academic advisor who helps with course selection, degree planning, and academic goal setting. Students must meet with their advisor at least once per semester before registering for courses. Advisors can also refer students to campus resources for academic support. Graduate students work with faculty advisors in their specific programs who provide guidance on thesis or dissertation research.

Research Opportunities
Undergraduate students can participate in faculty-led research projects through the Undergraduate Research Program. Students can earn academic credit or receive stipends for research work. The university hosts an annual Undergraduate Research Symposium where students present their research findings. Graduate students are encouraged to present research at regional and national conferences, with travel funding available through departmental budgets and the Graduate Student Association.
