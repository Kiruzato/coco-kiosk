Information Technology Services - Springfield University

Network and Wi-Fi Access
All students receive network access credentials upon enrollment. The campus-wide Wi-Fi network "SpringfieldU-Secure" provides high-speed internet access in all academic buildings, residence halls, and outdoor spaces. Students must authenticate using their university username and password. Guest Wi-Fi is available through the "SpringfieldU-Guest" network. The IT Help Desk can assist with connection issues Monday through Friday from 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM and weekends from 12:00 PM to 5:00 PM.

Email and Microsoft 365
Every student receives a university email address (username@springfield.edu) with 50GB of cloud storage. The university uses Microsoft 365, which includes access to Word, Excel, PowerPoint, Teams, OneDrive, and OneNote. Students can install Office applications on up to 5 devices. Email accounts remain active for one year after graduation.

Software and Site Licenses
Students have free access to specialized software including MATLAB, Adobe Creative Cloud, SPSS, AutoCAD, and various programming tools. Software can be downloaded from the IT Portal at software.springfield.edu. Site licenses are available for Windows and macOS operating systems. Virtual labs provide remote access to specialized engineering and design software.

Computer Labs
The university maintains 8 computer labs across campus equipped with both Windows and Mac computers. The Main Computer Lab in the Library has 120 workstations and is open 24/7 during the semester. Specialized labs include the Media Production Lab (video editing), Engineering Lab (CAD software), and Data Science Lab (statistical software). Students can print to any campus printer from lab computers.

IT Help Desk and Support
The IT Help Desk is located on the first floor of the Administration Building. Services include password resets, software installation assistance, virus removal, and troubleshooting. Students can submit support tickets online or call extension 4357 (HELP). Average response time for tickets is 24 hours. Emergency issues are addressed immediately.

Cybersecurity and Account Security
Students are required to enable two-factor authentication (2FA) for all university accounts. The IT Department provides security awareness training and sends monthly security bulletins. Students should report phishing emails to security@springfield.edu. Never share your password with anyone. The university will never ask for your password via email.

Online Learning Platform
The university uses Canvas as its Learning Management System (LMS). All courses have a Canvas site where instructors post syllabi, assignments, grades, and announcements. Students can access Canvas at canvas.springfield.edu or through the Canvas mobile app. The IT Help Desk provides Canvas support, or students can contact Online Learning Support at canvas-help@springfield.edu.

Technology Checkout Program
The library offers a technology checkout program where students can borrow laptops, tablets, cameras, audio recorders, and other equipment. Laptops can be checked out for up to 7 days. Other equipment has a 3-day checkout period. Students must have a valid student ID and be in good standing. Reservations can be made online through the library portal.

Cloud Storage and Backups
Students receive 1TB of OneDrive cloud storage through their Microsoft 365 account. The university recommends backing up all important files to cloud storage. Network drives (H: drive) provide an additional 25GB of storage accessible from any campus computer. IT performs nightly backups of network drives. Students are responsible for backing up files on personal devices.

Acceptable Use Policy
Students must comply with the university's Acceptable Use Policy when using IT resources. Prohibited activities include downloading copyrighted material, accessing inappropriate content, sharing account credentials, and using university resources for commercial purposes. Violations may result in loss of network access or disciplinary action. The full policy is available at policy.springfield.edu/aup.
