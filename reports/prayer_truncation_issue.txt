ISSUE REPORT: Prayer to Saint Columban Truncation
==================================================
Date: 2026-01-31
Phase: Post-Phase 21 (Semantic Trust Injection)
Status: Diagnosed, pending fix

SUMMARY
-------
The "Prayer to Saint Columban" response is incomplete. The chatbot returns only
the first portion of the prayer, missing the final sentence.

OBSERVED BEHAVIOR
-----------------
Query: "prayer to saint columban"

Returned (truncated):
"O blessed Columban...and the faith and love that helped you to endure
joyfully all the difficulties of time because of your love for Christ."

Expected (full prayer):
"O blessed Columban...and the faith and love that helped you to endure
joyfully all the difficulties of time because of your love for Christ.
Help Us, O beloved Columban, to live for Christ and not for ourselves
so that, when our mission is done, we can share with you the fullness
of life in heaven, through Christ our Lord, Amen."

Missing content: "Help Us, O beloved Columban...through Christ our Lord, Amen."

ROOT CAUSE
----------
The prayer was incorrectly chunked during PDF ingestion, splitting it across
4 separate chunks with inconsistent metadata:

  Chunk 647 | Section: "Prayer to St. Columban" | 584 chars
  Content: "prayer to st. columban o blessed columban...helped you to
           endure joyfully all the"

  Chunk 648 | Section: "General Information" | 53 chars
  Content: "difficulties of time because of your love for christ."

  Chunk 649 | Section: "General Information" | 150 chars
  Content: "help us, o beloved columban, to live for christ and not
           for ourselves so that, when our mission is done, we can
           share with you the fullness of life in"

  Chunk 650 | Section: "General Information" | 42 chars
  Content: "heaven, through christ our lord, amen. 135"

Problems:
1. Prayer split mid-sentence across 4 chunks
2. Chunks 648-650 have wrong section label ("General Information")
3. Chunks 648-650 are very short fragments (42-150 chars)

WHY CURRENT PIPELINE FAILS
--------------------------
1. RETRIEVAL: Only chunk 647 appears in top-K results
   - Chunks 648-650 have low semantic similarity to "prayer to saint columban"
   - Their incorrect section metadata ("General Information") hurts ranking
   - They're too short to score well in hybrid ranking

2. PHASE 19 EXPANSION: Adds only +/-1 neighbors
   - Chunk 647 expands to include 646 and 648
   - Chunk 646 is "appendix d" (useless header, 10 chars)
   - Chunk 648 adds partial content but 649-650 are NOT included
   - Result: Only gets up to "...because of your love for christ."

3. LLM: Correctly outputs what it receives (incomplete context)

AFFECTED FILES
--------------

- Source PDF: 2022_student_manual_latest.pdf (Appendix D section)
