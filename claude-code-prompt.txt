Example:
Voice Input Query:
What is 5 * 5 - 5 +? 5 + 23.

1.	Calculate \(5 * 5\):

\[
5 * 5 = 25
\]
2.	Now substitute that back into the expression:

\[
25 - 5 + 5 + 23
\]
3.	Next, perform the subtraction:

\[
25 - 5 = 20
\]
4.	Now add \(5\):

\[
20 + 5 = 25
\]
5.	Finally, add \(23\):

\[
25 + 23 = 48
\]
So, the final answer is \(48\).
