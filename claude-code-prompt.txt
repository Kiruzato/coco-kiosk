Prompt
We are observing recurring issues with arithmetic queries, especially when they originate from voice input.
Example:
User:
What is 5 times 5 times 2 minus 100 times 5 thousand?
STT output:
5 * 5. * 2 - 100 * 5,000.
Chatbot response:
To solve the expression 5 times 5 times 2 minus 100 times 5,000, we first calculate each part. First, calculate 5 times 5 times 2: - 5 times 5 equals 25 - 25 times 2 equals 50 Next, calculate 100 times 5,000: - 100 times 5,000 equals 500,000 Now, subtract the two results: - 50 minus 500,000 equals -499,950 So, the final answer is -499,950.
Observed problems:
•	STT punctuation breaks parsing.
•	Expressions are evaluated without normalization or validation.
•	Output formatting collapses (bullets merge, spacing breaks).
•	Simple arithmetic responses are overly verbose.
•	Voice and text responses sometimes differ in formatting behavior.
